# Gallery

Launchers people built by describing them to an AI agent. Each entry is a folder with:

- `home.png`: a real screenshot of the home screen. Crop or hide anything personal, such as notifications and account
  names. Don't include the drawer, since it shows every app you have.
- `README.md`: the prompt you used, your agent, your phone or emulator, and anything you had to fix by hand.

Only real results, please: no mockups, and no edits that change how the launcher looks.
